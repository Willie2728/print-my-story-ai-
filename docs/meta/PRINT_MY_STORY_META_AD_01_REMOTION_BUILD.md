# PRINT MY STORY — META AD #1 REMOTION BUILD BRIEF

## Purpose
Create the first paid Meta video ad for **Print My Story AI** as a finished upload-ready MP4. This is the primary creative for the launch campaign and should be built in Remotion, rendered, reviewed, and exported for Meta Ads Manager.

## Final deliverable
- Filename: `Print-My-Story-Meta-Ad-01-Hero.mp4`
- Duration: **15 seconds**
- Aspect ratio: **9:16 vertical**
- Resolution: **1080 x 1920**
- Frame rate: **30 fps**
- Codec/container: **H.264 MP4**
- Audio: AAC, stereo, normalized for mobile playback
- Safe-zone aware for Instagram/Facebook Reels and Stories
- All important text must remain away from top/bottom UI overlays
- Captions burned in and readable on a phone

## Campaign concept
**Your Child Is the Hero**

The ad should communicate the product in seconds: a parent can create a personalized storybook in which their child becomes the hero, choose a genre/story world, add personal details, upload a photo, have AI stylize the photo into cover art, preview the book, and continue to checkout.

The emotion is the product. The viewer should immediately imagine their own child opening a book and seeing themselves inside the story.

## Primary hook
On-screen text during the opening 1–2 seconds:

**“What if THEY were the hero?”**

Voiceover may say:

**“What if your child opened a book… and discovered they were the hero?”**

## 15-second scene plan

### Scene 1 — Hook / emotional reveal — 0.0–2.5 sec
Visual:
- A joyful child opens a beautiful personalized storybook.
- Parent is beside them.
- Child reacts with delight and recognition.
- Warm, premium, magical family atmosphere.

On-screen text:
**WHAT IF THEY WERE THE HERO?**

Motion:
- Fast push-in or gentle parallax.
- No logo intro.
- Product/emotion must appear immediately.

### Scene 2 — Choose their world — 2.5–5.0 sec
Visual montage, fast cuts around 0.4–0.6 sec each:
- Fairytale castle
- Superhero city
- Outer space
- Safari/jungle
- Sports/championship

On-screen microcopy:
**Pick their world.**

Show that Print My Story supports many genres and is not comedy-only.

### Scene 3 — Personalize — 5.0–8.5 sec
Visual:
- Parent enters the child's name and a few personal details in the Print My Story interface.
- Show the microphone/voice-answer option briefly.
- Show the new cover personalization step:
  1. Upload/take a photo
  2. AI stylizes it into a storybook/cartoon cover

On-screen text sequence:
**Add their name.**
**Tell us what makes them special.**
**Turn their photo into cover art.**

Do not linger on UI. The flow should feel effortless.

### Scene 4 — The story appears — 8.5–11.5 sec
Visual:
- Personalized pages animate into view.
- Child's name is visibly integrated into the story.
- Show a polished personalized book cover generated from the uploaded photo.
- Show preview/edit capability quickly.

On-screen text:
**Their name. Their adventure. Their story.**

### Scene 5 — Emotional close + CTA — 11.5–15.0 sec
Visual:
- Parent and child reading together and smiling.
- Finished personalized book visible.
- End on clean branded card.

End card copy:
**PRINT MY STORY AI**
**Make them the hero.**

CTA button graphic:
**CREATE THEIR STORY**

Optional secondary line:
**Preview before you buy.**

## Voiceover script
Target natural read: ~15 seconds.

> “What if your child opened a book and discovered they were the hero? Pick their world, add their name and what makes them special, even turn their photo into personalized cover art. Their name. Their adventure. Their story. Create theirs with Print My Story.”

If the full script feels rushed, prioritize clarity and use this shorter version:

> “What if your child became the hero of their own story? Pick their world, add their name, upload a photo, and let Print My Story create a personalized book just for them. Create their story today.”

## Visual direction
- Warm
- Magical
- Premium
- Emotional
- Family-safe
- Lively rather than slow
- Modern commercial quality
- Avoid stock-video feel where possible
- Avoid generic AI morphing or inconsistent faces
- Maintain character continuity across scenes
- Use one consistent parent/child pair for the emotional scenes
- If AI-generated visuals are used, faces, clothing, hair, age, and skin tone must stay consistent
- Avoid uncanny hands, unreadable book text, warped covers, or morphing faces

## Family casting direction
Use a joyful, photogenic Black parent and a very cheerful baby/young child with attractive natural curly hair. Expressions should feel authentic, warm, happy, playful, and aspirational. The child should look delighted, not posed or expressionless.

## Brand / UI styling
Use the current Print My Story palette:
- Deep navy/slate
- Sky blue
- Cyan
- White
- Avoid the old dull brown palette
- Avoid default purple SaaS gradients

Typography:
- Bold, clean, premium sans serif
- Large enough for mobile viewing
- Strong contrast
- No more than one main message per shot

## Music / sound design
Use a lively, fun, enchanted storybook track.
- Bright
- Playful
- Modern
- Magical
- Light percussion
- Pizzicato/plucked or marimba-like accents are acceptable
- Do NOT use a sleepy nursery tune
- Do NOT use anything ominous
- Music should support the emotion without overpowering voiceover
- Mix voiceover clearly above music
- Add subtle page-flip, sparkle, and reveal SFX

## Remotion implementation requirements
1. Use Remotion for the final build.
2. Create a composition named something like `PrintMyStoryMetaAd01`.
3. Use 30 fps and 450 frames total for 15 seconds.
4. 1080 x 1920 composition.
5. Keep each scene modular so later variants can swap hooks, CTA, copy, images, or footage.
6. Use `Sequence` / scene components for timing.
7. Use smooth but fast transitions; no slow cinematic intro.
8. Avoid excessive animation. Text motion should support readability.
9. All text must remain inside Meta-safe zones.
10. Add subtitles/captions as burned-in text even if voiceover is present.
11. Ensure the ad works with sound off: the viewer should understand the product from visuals + on-screen text alone.
12. Render the final MP4 and also render one still thumbnail from the strongest emotional frame.

## Required assets / asset strategy
If the repository already contains approved Print My Story screenshots/assets, use them.
