Print My Story · PMS-DOC-001 · no-contact buyer aid

Make it personal without making it private.

A memorable gift needs recognizable details, not every detail. Use this card before generating or approving a book to decide what belongs in the story and what should stay out.

Good personalization inputs

  • Inside jokes you would say in front of the recipient.
  • Favorite routines, hobbies, sayings, harmless quirks and shared memories.
  • Achievements or affectionate stories the recipient would recognize.
  • Specific details that make the story feel unmistakably theirs.

Skip or rewrite sensitive details

  • Passwords, API keys or security answers.
  • Financial account or government-ID information.
  • Health or highly private family information.
  • Employer/client confidential material.
  • Anything you would not want printed, gifted or read aloud.

Gift-ready check

  • Does it feel recognizably about them?
  • Does the tone fit the relationship?
  • Is at least one detail specific instead of generic?
  • Would you be comfortable reading the finished passage aloud to them?

When in doubt

Skip the prompt or replace the detail with something lighter. Personalization quality should come from specificity and judgment—not pressure to disclose more.

Decision rule: a preview is gift-ready when it feels specific, tone-appropriate and comfortable to give—not when every prompt has been answered. A skipped prompt is a valid buyer choice.

Planning aid only. This card does not guarantee print quality, delivery, customer satisfaction or privacy compliance. Review the final book yourself before checkout.
