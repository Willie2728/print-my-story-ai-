## Customer Truth
A personalized story is more qualified for purchase when it feels recognizably about the recipient without requiring unnecessary private information.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Would they recognize the story if their name disappeared?

Before checkout, hide the name and read the preview again. Would someone close to the recipient still know who the story is about? Look for one safe, specific detail and the right relationship tone. If a line feels generic, edit it. Personalization quality is not how much private information you share; it is whether the story feels recognizably theirs.

**CTA:** Start the free preview. Use the Recognition Test. Edit one line before deciding whether the story earned checkout.
