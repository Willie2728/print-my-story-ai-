# Print My Story AI — Run 45

## Customer Truth
Personalization can create value, but customization friction can also create anxiety. The strongest currently demonstrable product loop is not a shipping promise; it is guided questions → AI-assisted draft → page-by-page preview/edit → an informed decision about whether to continue to checkout.

## Creative Strategy
**Campaign:** Start With the Preview.

**Hook:** Don’t make them design a book from a blank page. Give them something personal to react to.

**Body:** Pick the relationship. Answer the guided questions. Let the studio draft the story. Then turn the pages, change the lines, and decide whether it feels personal enough to continue. The buyer remains the editor before checkout.

**CTA:** Start Your Preview.

## Distribution Queue
Use short gift-occasion creative that demonstrates the low-friction sequence rather than making fulfillment promises: recipient → 10 guided questions → draft → preview → edit → optional checkout. Proposed campaign family: `print_my_story_start_with_preview`.

## Analytics / Evaluation
The key conversion question is whether preview-first creative increases starts of the creation flow and progression into preview relative to generic “buy a personalized book” messaging. Do not infer purchase, print, shipment, or delivery without order/provider records.

## Winner Library
No winner promotion without attributable behavior. No customer testimonial may be invented to manufacture social proof.

## Research Boundary
A study first published online August 30, 2026 in the *International Journal of Market Research* examines how perceived customization complexity, anxiety, and self-perceived design skill affect value and purchase intention in gifting. Use that as category evidence for reducing blank-page customization friction, not as a Print My Story result.
