# PRINT MY STORY AI — RUN123

## PMS-TXT-078 — Preview-ready is not print-ready

**Customer truth:** A generated story preview is not the same thing as a paid, accepted, printed, shipped, or delivered book.

**Hook:** Your story preview can be ready before your order exists.

**Body:** RUN123 changes the preview step from “Your book is ready” to “Your story preview is ready,” adds a visible claims boundary explaining that no order, payment, print job, shipment, or delivery exists until later checkout and fulfillment steps are actually confirmed, and changes the CTA from “Looks great — Checkout” to the neutral “Continue to checkout.”

**CTA:** Review every page, then continue to checkout only when the preview is right for the recipient.

## Production / claims boundary

- Production-ready copy; no external post or ad is claimed live.
- No video, image, or audio was rendered.
- A generated preview does not establish payment, order acceptance, print fulfillment, shipment, delivery, or customer satisfaction.
- Current verified baseline at creation: 0 StoryGrowthEvent records and 0 ordered Book records.

## Research context

Common Sense Media’s January 22, 2026 research found that more than eight in ten parents expressed concern about AI-enabled toys collecting children’s personal information. That is adjacent family-AI privacy context only; it is not evidence of demand or performance for personalized books.
