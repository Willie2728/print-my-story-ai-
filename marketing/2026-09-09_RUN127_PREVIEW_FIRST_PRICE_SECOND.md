# PRINT MY STORY RUN127 — Preview First, Price the Order Second

**Asset:** PMS-TXT-079  
**Customer Truth:** A gift buyer can love the creative concept and still abandon the order if shipping, tax, eligibility, or delivery expectations feel like a surprise at checkout.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Personalize the gift first. Price the actual order before you pay.

**Body:** Build the preview free and edit it until it feels gift-ready. When checkout is available, Stripe is where the final item price, shipping, tax, address eligibility, and delivery timing are confirmed. A preview is not a paid order, print job, shipment, or delivery promise.

**CTA:** Start the free preview; decide on the order only after checkout shows the real total and timing.
