# RUN159 — Print My Story One-Detail Gift Starter

## Customer Truth
PwC's Holiday Outlook 2026 reports that 64% of surveyed U.S. consumers are drawn to nostalgic, screen-free gifts, and 41% of people buying gifts for children in their family are leaning toward screen-free, hands-on activities. The useful conversion implication is not “AI gift” by itself; it is using AI to help create a specific physical gift that feels personal rather than generic. These are market signals, not Print My Story results.

## Creative Strategy
**PMS-TXT-085:** “Start with the detail only they would recognize.”

Conversion premise: reduce blank-page friction by asking for one harmless memory, phrase, quirk, or shared place—not a life history. The preview must pass an “only us” test before checkout.
